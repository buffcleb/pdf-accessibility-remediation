# What changed in the docs: 22 October 2026

[Documentation](README.md) › What changed, 22 October 2026

The documentation was checked against the app and the code on 22 October 2026, the first full review since 19
October. This page lists every substantive change, by reader. In the pages themselves, each changed section starts
with a line in bold:
- **Changed 22 October 2026:** the section was there before, and says something different now;
- **New 22 October 2026:** the section is new;
- **Updated 22 October 2026 (#N):** in the evaluation documents, a finding's status since the document was written.
  The finding itself is left as it was written.

Eight screenshots were retaken ([which ones](images/README.md)).

## For everyone using the service

- **Uploading:** while a large upload is checked, the form says what it's waiting for, and that a file already
  received carries on if you leave the page. The same file sent again with the same choices, while the first is
  waiting or in progress, is refused and uses no pages (#560).
  [Submitting documents](user-guide/people/submitting-documents.md)
- **Lists** (documents, review items, help requests, people, groups, reports) have one search-and-filter bar and a
  pager with First, Previous, Next and Last, above and below the list. Your documents can be filtered by how they were
  **Sent**, by a script or in the web app.
  [Your results](user-guide/people/your-results.md), [Submitting documents](user-guide/people/submitting-documents.md)
- **A document's page:**
  - **Previous** and **Next** follow the list you opened it from;
  - the **Downloads** card has **Download everything (zip)**, with **One file at a time** folded under it;
  - the music and math files have their new names;
  - **Show** is on every review item with a page, and highlights its area.

  [Your results](user-guide/people/your-results.md)
- **The tag manager:**
  - a new tag can be chosen before it's saved, and its type or alt text changed;
  - details and alt text can always be read in full;
  - an item opened from the review list is outlined on its page.
  - in One page view, the page is turned as in a PDF viewer: up and down arrows, then the page number box.

  [Tag manager](user-guide/people/tag-manager/README.md),
  [Finding your way](user-guide/people/tag-manager/finding-your-way.md),
  [Areas](user-guide/people/tag-manager/areas.md)
- **Uploads refused by the malware check**, where the service scans uploads, and what to do.
  [Troubleshooting](user-guide/people/troubleshooting.md)
- **API tokens** for scripts: **Confirm sending automatically**, and that a group's admins can revoke a token.
  [Email and settings](user-guide/people/email-and-settings.md), [Glossary](user-guide/glossary.md)
- **After the service is updated**, a page opened before reloads once, or offers **Reload**.
  [Troubleshooting](user-guide/people/troubleshooting.md)

## Found while reviewing

These are problems in the app or the deployment, not in the docs. Each is fixed separately.
- **ClamAV couldn't reach the egress proxy:** the proxy's network policy didn't let it in, so with `EGRESS_PROXY` on,
  its signatures couldn't update.
- **Layout:**
  - Settings' **Sign out of all devices** button sits against its card's edge;
  - the Quality report shows its row count twice, and its chart's labels run past the card;
  - the page ledger's dates wrap onto several lines.
