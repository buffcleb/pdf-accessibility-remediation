# What changed in the docs: 23 October 2026

[Documentation](README.md) › What changed, 23 October 2026

The documentation for the new **service admin** role (#564), a role between group admin and super admin. This isn't
a full review: the [review of 22 October](changes-2026-10-22.md) still stands for everything else. System's
**Versions** tab (#570) and the next click after an update loading the new web app (#575) are the other changes. In the pages, each changed section starts with **Changed 23
October 2026** or **New 23 October 2026**.

One new screenshot, `service-admin.png` ([images](images/README.md)).

## For service admins

- **A guide of their own:** who they are, how someone becomes one, the Administration menu, a table of what's theirs
  and what stays with super admins, the emails they get (with the new **Service alerts** choice), and what the
  service records about their work.
  [Guide for service admins](user-guide/service-admins/README.md)

## For super admins

- **Making a service admin:** **People (all groups) → Manage → Make service admin**, the role filter, and what a
  service admin can and can't do. [Super admins](user-guide/super-admins/README.md#service-admins),
  [Groups and people](user-guide/super-admins/groups-and-people.md#people-all-groups)
- **Shared with service admins:** help requests, the reports only staff see, the audit log; not pausing queues, not
  Grafana. [Help requests](user-guide/super-admins/help-requests.md), [Queues](user-guide/super-admins/queues.md),
  [System](user-guide/super-admins/system.md#grafana), [Reports and audit](user-guide/super-admins/reports-and-audit.md)
- **Check only per AI level:** an AI level's **Audits** can now be **Disabled**, so Check only isn't offered with it
  (#586). [Services and AI levels](user-guide/super-admins/services-and-ai-levels.md#ai-levels)
- **System › Versions:** every workload's release, digest and pin is a tab of its own, beside **Now** and **Over
  time** (#570). [System](user-guide/super-admins/system.md#versions)

## For everyone using the service

- **Settings:** the **Service alerts** emails (service admins only), and who gets the **Service summary**.
  [Email and settings](user-guide/people/email-and-settings.md)
- **Moving to another group:** a service admin can do it too. [Signing in](user-guide/people/signing-in.md)

## For whoever runs the service

- **Growing a volume:** raise its size in `deploy.env` (`DB_SIZE`, `FILES_SIZE` and the rest) and `scripts/deploy.sh`
  grows it, after showing it in its plan; a smaller size stops the deploy. The manual steps, and what to do when the
  storage class can't grow a volume (#592). The "volume nearly full" alert now names the right setting.
  [Growing a volume](deployment/first-cluster/storage.md#growing-a-volume)
- **The "volume nearly full" alert's threshold** is a `deploy.env` setting, `VOLUME_ALERT_FREE_PERCENT` (10% by
  default), applied at the next deploy (#589). [Monitoring](operations/monitoring.md#settings)
- **The portal spec's roles table** has a service admin column, and says each route asks for a named permission.
  [Portal spec](design/portal-spec.md)
- **Security and privacy:** permissions per route and the route-by-role test, service admins' row-level security
  bypass and its audit, and their security alert. [Security and privacy](architecture/security-and-privacy.md)
- **Monitoring:** service admins get the service's alerts unless they turn them off; security alerts stay with super
  admins. A new security alert, "A new service admin". [Monitoring](operations/monitoring.md)
- **Rolling back:** what a downgrade past migration 0048 loses; open pages load the earlier web app on the next click
  (#575). [Rolling back](operations/rollback.md)
- **The web app on a Worker:** `_headers` keeps `/build.json` uncached (#575).
  [Cloudflare](deployment/cloudflare.md#the-web-app-on-a-cloudflare-worker)
- **The code map:** `portal/permissions.py`. [Source map](architecture/source-map.md)
