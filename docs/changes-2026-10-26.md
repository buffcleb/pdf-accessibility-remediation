# What changed in the docs: 26 October 2026

[Documentation](README.md) › What changed, 26 October 2026

The documentation for **subgroups** (#431): groups inside a group, one level deep, using the parent's pages and
following its settings; and signing review items off in the tag manager (#663). This isn't a full review: the [review of 22 October](changes-2026-10-22.md) still stands for
everything else. In the pages, each changed section starts with **New 26 October 2026** or **added 26 October 2026**.

## For group admins

- **A page of its own:** adding a subgroup, who joins it (sign-in order, moving people), its pages and limit, which
  settings follow the parent and how to set them back, local-only, and reports with the **Group** filter.
  [Subgroups](user-guide/group-admins/subgroups.md)
- **Common tasks:** adding a subgroup, limiting its pages, moving someone into it.
  [Guide for group admins](user-guide/group-admins/README.md#common-tasks)

## For people

- **Signing review items off in the tag manager** (#663): the item's status is on its highlight and beside it in

## For super admins
