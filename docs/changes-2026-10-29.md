# What changed in the docs: 29 October 2026

[Documentation](README.md) › What changed, 29 October 2026

The documentation for **new tags before the save** (#692): the tags your unsaved changes make (from an area, the AI's
suggestions, a split, a figure's parts, a list or table built) are tags like any other until you save, and the
content of a tag you've deleted can be tagged again in the same save. This isn't a full review: the
[review of 22 October](changes-2026-10-22.md) still stands for everything else. In the pages, each changed section
starts with **New 29 October 2026** or **Updated 28 October 2026**.

**No AI** (#659), also documented today: a remediation choice for documents that mustn't go to any AI, offered by
super admins and then by each group. Changed sections start with **New 29 October 2026**.

## For people

- **New tags are tags:** their row in the tag tree (marked **new tag**), their outline, their menu and the editing
  panel; taking one back takes the changes made to it, and **Undo** brings them back.
  [The new tag, before you save](user-guide/people/tag-manager/areas.md#the-new-tag-before-you-save),
  [Saving your changes](user-guide/people/tag-manager/saving.md)
- **A deleted tag's content** can go into another tag or a new one before you save.
  [Areas: tagged content](user-guide/people/tag-manager/areas.md#tagged-content),
  [Deleting](user-guide/people/tag-manager/merge-split-delete.md#deleting)

## For developers

- **Edits naming tags other edits make:** the `id` and `{"made", "group"}` names, how the engine finds tags as each
  edit's turn comes, and `web/src/viewer/made.ts`.
  [The tag manager and editor](architecture/tag-editor.md) ("Tags an edit makes, before the save")

## No AI (#659)

- **For people:** picking **No AI** at upload, what the document page and report say, and no AI in the tag manager
  afterwards. [Choosing the AI](user-guide/people/submitting-documents.md#choosing-the-ai),
  [Your results](user-guide/people/your-results.md#notes), [AI review](user-guide/people/tag-manager/saving.md#ai-review)
- **For group admins:** offering No AI, preselecting it, or making it the only choice.
  [No AI](user-guide/group-admins/group-settings.md#no-ai)
- **For super admins:** **Let groups offer No AI** on Settings › Limits › AI.
  [Settings](user-guide/super-admins/settings.md)
- **For developers:** the `none` level key, `groups.no_ai` (migration 0054), provider `none` for any document
  without a level, and the `no_ai_document` refusals.
  [Services, AI levels and pages](architecture/services-and-quotas.md)
