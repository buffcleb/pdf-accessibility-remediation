# Glossary

Terms used in the portal and these guides, in alphabetical order.

**Accessibility check.** The PDF/UA-1 check made with veraPDF, before and after remediation. A document
"passes" when no machine-checkable rule fails. It can't judge whether alt text is accurate or headings are the
right ones, which is why there's also a review list.

**AI level.** Which AI helps with your document: for example *Local AI* (on the service's own servers),
*Cloud AI* or *Commercial AI*. Your administrators decide which levels you can use. You never need to know the
model behind a level.

**Alt text (alternative text).** A short description of an image, read aloud by screen readers instead of the
image. The AI writes a first draft for every figure; charts and diagrams are worth checking.

**Artifact.** Content that's decoration rather than meaning (page numbers, running headers, borders, a scanned
page's background). Screen readers skip it.

**Audit (Check only).** A check of a document without changing it: the accessibility check and the review list,
but no remediated file.

**Group.** The people a service is shared with, usually a department or organization. You join yours
automatically by your email address.

**Kind of document.** Within an AI level, a kind such as *Math and science* or *Forms* picks the AI best suited
to that kind of document. *General* suits everything else. *Let AI decide* picks the kind for you.

**Local-only.** A group setting that keeps documents on the service's own servers: anything that would send
them to an outside provider is unavailable.

**Needs review.** The list of things a machine can't be sure about, with a severity (high, review or suggestion)
and a suggested action.

**OCR (optical character recognition).** Reading the text in a scanned page, which is otherwise just a picture.
The service adds an invisible text layer so the scanned text can be read aloud, searched and copied.

**Pages (allowance).** Each page of each document counts against your allowance for each processing option and
AI level it uses. Your group has a pool; you may also have your own limit.

**PDF/UA.** The ISO standard for accessible PDF (ISO 14289). PDF/UA-1 is the version this service aims at.

**Processing option (service).** How documents are tagged: *Standard* (this service's own engine) or, if your
administrators offer it, *Adobe Autotag* (Adobe PDF Services).

**Reading order.** The order a screen reader reads a page in. On multi-column or complex pages it's worked out
from the layout, with AI help where the layout is unclear.

**Remediate.** Make a document accessible: add tags, alt text, reading order, language and title, and fix what
can be fixed.

**Tags.** The hidden structure of an accessible PDF: which text is a heading, a paragraph, a list, a table cell
or a figure, and in what order. The tag manager shows them.

**Tag manager and editor.** The portal's side-by-side view of two versions of a document, where you can check
every tag against the page and fix it.

**Two files.** When a document can't pass without a visible change (usually because of a font), you get the
visually identical file and a modified one that passes, and choose.

**Version.** Each saved edit, corrected file or support fix is a new version; the original results are never
changed.
