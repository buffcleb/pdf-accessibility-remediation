# Documents and help requests

[Guide for group admins](README.md) › Documents and help requests

## The group's documents

**My group → Documents** lists everyone's documents in the group, a page at a time, with who submitted them, how
each was processed (audit only, the AI level), the result and the status. Open one to see its page, exactly as
its owner does.

- **Search** by file name or by a person's email address, pick a **status**, and use **Filters** for the result,
  the processing service or AI level, audits, and the dates submitted. The filters in use are shown as chips.
- Click **Document**, **User**, **Submitted**, **Pages** or **Result** to sort; choose how many to show per page
  at the bottom. The view is in the page's address, so a link to it, or Back from a document, returns to the same
  place.

- **Opening someone else's files is recorded** in the audit log, with who and when.
- You can open the tag manager and **edit** your group's documents, as their owner can. Each save is a new version;
  the original results are never changed.
- What the AI cost isn't shown to group admins; pages are.
- Under a document's name, **Files removed** or **Files go …** shows that its owner marked it done (or that a super
  admin removed its files). Only the owner, or a super admin, can remove a document's files early.

## Help requests

**My group → Help requests** lists your group's requests: what was asked, about which document, and the status.

- You can **read** the conversation and **reply**; the person is emailed.
- Changing the status (taking it, waiting on the person, finishing it) is done by super admins, who handle the
  requests.

People in your group ask for help from the document's page; see [Getting help](../people/getting-help.md).
