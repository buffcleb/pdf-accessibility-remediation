# Documents and help requests

[Guide for group admins](README.md) › Documents and help requests

## The group's documents

**My group → Documents** lists everyone's documents in the group, with who submitted them, the result and the
status. Open one to see its page, exactly as its owner does.

- **Opening someone else's files is recorded** in the audit log, with who and when.
- You can open the tag viewer and **edit** your group's documents, as their owner can. Each save is a new version;
  the original results are never changed.
- What the AI cost isn't shown to group admins; pages are.

## Help requests

**My group → Help requests** lists your group's requests: what was asked, about which document, and the status.

- You can **read** the conversation and **reply**; the person is emailed.
- Changing the status (taking it, waiting on the person, finishing it) is done by super admins, who handle the
  requests.

People in your group ask for help from the document's page; see [Getting help](../people/getting-help.md).
