# Hearing it read aloud

[The tag manager](README.md) › Hearing it read aloud

**Read aloud** reads a version's tags in reading order with your browser's voices, outlining each tag as it's read.
It's a quick way to hear order and alt-text problems.

![The read-aloud bar at the top of the tag manager: a Version choice set to "Right: Remediated", previous, Play and
next buttons, "Go to the selected tag", a Speed choice set to Normal, and Close. Under them: "1 of 40, page 1:
Northfield University", and the keys K, J and L.](../../../images/tag-manager/read-aloud.png)

- Choose the left or right version (the right one includes your unsaved changes), the speed, and play, pause,
  previous and next, or start from the selected tag.
- Keys: **K** play or pause, **J** previous, **L** next.
- Its controls sit in the heading at the top, which stays in view, so they and the tag being read are seen
  together.
- **Read aloud from here** in the tag menu starts reading at once from that tag (for a list or section, from where
  it starts).

## What it says

It reads roughly as a screen reader would: "Heading level 2, …", "Figure: <alt text>", "List, 4 items", "Table, 3
rows, 2 columns", "Link: …". Alt text and read-aloud text are read in place of the content, artifacts are skipped,
and a tag's language picks the voice when your system has one.

It isn't a screen reader: NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver word things differently, so check with one before you finish.
